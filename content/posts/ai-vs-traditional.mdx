## Time Investment

The brief makes it clear that AI tooling drastically shortens the drafting loop. Instead of waiting a week for a writer, you can go from prompt to polished draft in under an hour. Traditional coaching still wins for white-glove service, but the roadmap recommends leading with AI tutorials because they deliver instant activation for searchers.

## Cost Analysis

Traditional resume writers often charge $300-$800 per engagement. The keyword research doc positions AI builders as the "free or freemium" hook, especially around Tier 4 keywords like **"free AI resume builder no sign up."** Pairing AI-generated drafts with optional expert reviews lets you capture both budget-conscious and premium buyers.

## Quality & Customization

AI tools excel at structured consistency, ATS compliance, and fast keyword iteration. Human writers shine when you need nuanced storytelling or highly niche positioning (executive, federal, academic CVs). The recommended content outline proposes comparison tables that highlight where each method wins so readers can self-select.

## When to Use Each Method

- **Choose AI** for rapid testing, multi-version resumes, or when you want to experiment with profession-specific prompts such as healthcare or remote work.
- **Choose traditional writing** when you need deep narrative coaching, interview prep bundles, or compliance-heavy industries.

Close the article with a CTA like "Start with AI, add an expert review when you need it." That matches the conversion strategy in the deck and keeps both keyword clusters—DIY builders and done-for-you shoppers—inside the same funnel.
